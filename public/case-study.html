THE CHALLENGE & THE SOLUTION

One place for the moving parts.

Small service businesses often juggle spreadsheets, inboxes, and manual reminders. This project demonstrates how a focused full-stack application can bring those operations together and reduce repetitive admin tasks.

01.

Manage every lead

Create, edit, search, filter, qualify, and convert opportunities inside an interactive pipeline.

02.

Know your customers

Customer records link to projects, and winning a lead creates its customer record once.

03.

Deliver projects on time

Organize projects and tasks, track progress based on actual completion, and manage deadlines.

04.

Automate the busywork

Four real backend workflows handle inquiry capture, due follow-ups, conversion, and completion.

05.

Watch the numbers

Live counters and custom SVG charts reflect the actual records inside each visitor's demo.

06.

Audit every workflow

View execution results, origin (automatic, scheduled, manual), notifications, and activity history.

THE PRODUCT

Purposeful design. Real interactions.

See your sales pipeline clearly.

Opportunity value, priority, and stages are easy to scan at a glance. Every card opens an editor for real, persistent changes. Accessible status controls work without drag-and-drop.

Automations you can inspect.

Each workflow shows its trigger, action, enabled state, and logged execution history. The manual follow-up trigger executes the real server workflow and prevents duplicates.

Designed for smaller screens, too.

Collapsible navigation, responsive data cards, readable charts, mobile forms, and touch-friendly controls keep the experience practical across devices.
